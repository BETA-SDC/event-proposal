**Body:**

Dear all,

The tenth session of Beta Meet is on **Wednesday, November 4, 14:00–15:30, in H4-103**. The topic this time is failure in research.

Beta Meet is a series run by the Activities and Academic Departments of the SDC, Beta College. For this session, three students are talking about work of their own that didn't turn out:

- **Wang Yihan**, biology
- **Cheng Zizheng**, physics
- **Li Yiyao**, chemistry

In group meetings, defenses and poster sessions, people present the part that eventually worked. The part that didn't usually stays in a personal notebook, or gets complained about to a labmate and then dropped. Here it's reversed: 15 minutes each on one specific failure — what they saw, how they worked out that something was wrong, what they did about it, and where, looking back, they could have caught it earlier.

The floor opens for questions afterwards. If you're stuck on something right now — an experiment you can't reproduce, a direction that feels shakier the longer you work on it, a project you're not sure you should keep — ask.
