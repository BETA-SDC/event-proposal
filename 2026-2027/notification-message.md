Dear students:

During the midterm phase, everyone faces a lot of study pressure. To help everyone relax after the midterm exam, the β College bean hunting is coming!

Colorful little beans, a vast world in a small space. Pick up the tweezers, gently place them, and watch the scattered colors slowly gather in your hand, turning into cute cartoons, healing landscapes, and exclusive letters... In that moment, your heart quiets down. No prior experience needed—just come and you'll get the hang of it right away.
